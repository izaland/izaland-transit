The Wataiga Monorail is a private straddle-beam monorail connecting the Watarui district (southern terminus at Otsuminiswae) with the urban centre of Igattarun, terminating at Igattarun Juwon, the city's central station. The line threads through a residential corridor including the Aserimowa housing area, the Bonkado hospital district, and the Wataiga welfare university campus.

All 13 stations are served by every train, with a minimum frequency of 6 trains per hour throughout the day and up to 12 trains per hour during peak periods. The line operates from 05:30 to 23:30 seven days a week. The northern terminus (WI01) provides interchange with both the IZX (M13) and the WKB commuter line, making it an important gateway into the broader Izarail network.
